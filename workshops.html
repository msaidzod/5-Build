Workshops

Hands-on learning in editing, cinematography, and storytelling.

Build Your Filmmaking Skills

Explore the filmmaking process through practical exercises. Develop a story, experiment with camera techniques, and learn how editing brings individual shots together. Beginners and experienced creators can learn from one another.

Workshop Topics

  • Screenwriting: develop characters and structure a story.
  • Cinematography: practice framing, lighting, and movement.
  • Sound: explore clear dialogue and background audio.
  • Editing: arrange footage to create rhythm and meaning.

Practice the Process

  1. Write a short scene with a clear beginning and ending.
  2. Plan your shots using a simple storyboard.
  3. Record the scene with a camera or smartphone.
  4. Edit your footage and share it for feedback.

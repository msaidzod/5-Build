Collaborations

Connect with other creatives to bring ideas to life.

Create Together

Filmmaking brings together people with different skills and perspectives. Writers, camera operators, editors, performers, and sound designers can contribute to a shared creative vision while learning from one another.

Ways to Collaborate

  • Develop a screenplay with a writing partner.
  • Form a small crew to produce a short film.
  • Exchange feedback on rough cuts and story ideas.
  • Create music or sound effects for a film project.
  • Help design costumes, props, or filming locations.

Plan Your First Project

  1. Share your idea and explain the story you want to tell.
  2. Identify the skills and equipment your project needs.
  3. Agree on team roles and a realistic production schedule.
  4. Discuss credit, permissions, and how work will be shared.
  5. Check in regularly and give constructive feedback.

Want to develop your skills before joining a project? Explore our workshops. For inspiration, visit our film screenings page.
